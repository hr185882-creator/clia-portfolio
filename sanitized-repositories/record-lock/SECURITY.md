# Security Policy

## Supported material

This policy applies to the sanitized public reference kit. Production deployments may use additional controls that are intentionally not documented here.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not disclose active vulnerabilities, credentials, private personal information, restricted source files, sealed records, or unlawful content through a public issue.

Report security concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate private contact channel. Include:

- the affected record, file, page, or component;
- a concise description of the issue;
- safe reproduction steps;
- the potential impact;
- whether analytical integrity or record history can be altered.

## High-priority reports

The following should be treated as urgent:

- exposed credentials or deployment tokens;
- unauthorized access to restricted source packages;
- modification of released records without a revision event;
- state changes that bypass human review;
- source-identifier collisions or broken provenance links;
- injection or script-execution vulnerabilities;
- public export of restricted or withheld fields.

## Safe-harbor boundary

Testing must avoid privacy harm, data destruction, service disruption, credential access, social engineering, and retrieval or redistribution of unlawful or restricted material. Stop testing once sufficient evidence has been collected to report the issue safely.
