# Security Policy

## Supported material

This policy applies to the sanitized public reference kit. The production deployment may use additional controls that are intentionally not documented here.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not disclose active vulnerabilities, credentials, private personal information, victim-identifying material, sealed records, or unlawful content through a public issue.

Report security concerns privately to the repository owner through an appropriate private contact channel. Include:

- the affected file, page, or component;
- a concise description of the issue;
- safe reproduction steps;
- the potential impact;
- any evidence that sensitive data is exposed.

## High-priority reports

The following should be treated as urgent:

- exposed credentials or deployment tokens;
- victim-identifying or protected personal information;
- access to restricted source packages;
- injection or script-execution vulnerabilities;
- unauthorized modification of claim records;
- integrity failures that allow an unreviewed claim to appear verified;
- links to illegal or abusive material.

## Safe-harbor boundary

Testing must avoid privacy harm, data destruction, service disruption, credential access, social engineering, and retrieval or redistribution of unlawful material. Stop testing once sufficient evidence has been collected to report the issue safely.
