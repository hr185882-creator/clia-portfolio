# Cabinet Stability Forecast: Kristi Noem's DHS Tenure

**Author and lead analyst:** Hasan Raza Kazmi  
**Original forecast date:** January 16, 2026  
**Category:** Political-risk and strategic forecasting

## Decision question

What was the probability that DHS Secretary Kristi Noem would leave office across immediate, annual, and longer-term horizons?

## Method

The assessment combined:

- open-source political and institutional research;
- prediction-market data from Polymarket and Kalshi;
- competing-hypothesis analysis;
- short-, medium-, and long-term probability estimates;
- advocate-versus-skeptic red-team analysis;
- explicit indicators that would change the forecast.

## Forecast ranges

- **By March 31, 2026:** 14–15%
- **By the end of 2026:** 35–40%
- **Before 2027:** 45–52%

## Core assessment

The immediate removal risk was assessed as low because the impeachment effort lacked the institutional power required to remove her and she retained presidential support.

Longer-term risk was materially higher because of political volatility, accumulated controversies, possible investigations, Republican defections, and the administration's historical willingness to remove officials once their political liability exceeds their utility.

## Strongest case for retention

- direct alignment with the administration's core immigration agenda;
- continued presidential support;
- Republican control of Congress;
- public posture consistent with the administration's communications strategy.

## Strongest case for departure

- potential escalation of self-dealing allegations;
- sustained negative attention;
- possibility of a formal investigation;
- high administrative turnover;
- possibility of voluntary departure for future political ambitions.

## Revision indicators

The forecast specified four principal indicators:

1. Public criticism from the president.
2. A DOJ or congressional investigation into the relevant contract allegations.
3. A significant break in Republican support, particularly in the Senate.
4. A sharp and sustained movement in prediction-market pricing.

## Portfolio value

This case demonstrates time-bounded forecasting, explicit uncertainty, institutional analysis, red teaming, probability calibration, and predefined conditions for revision rather than retrospective narrative fitting.
